## Context

The README notes that the September 25 handoff had no required branch-protection gate. The "All gates" job in ci.yml was designed so that branch protection can require a single check, but nothing confirms that the check is required.

## Alternatives

1. Only add the job to ci.yml and "All gates", and move branch protection to a separate task

## Decision

Add the Playwright job to ci.yml and to the `needs` of "All gates". Make "All gates" a required status check for `master`, and document that setting with a date.

## Reason

An obligatory gate is the point of the feature. Without a required check, a red "All gates" does not block merge.

## Consequences

Needs repository-admin rights. Because the setting lives outside the code, it is verified and recorded separately from the PR. Every existing gate also becomes merge-blocking.
